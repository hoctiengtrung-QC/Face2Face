The top ten reasons for happiness
Adapted from the Daily Mail
Rank 1
YOUR GENES
Some people are simply born happier than others. In a study of 4,000 adult twins, differences in their genes were the main reasons for their different levels of happiness.
Rank 2
BEING MARRIED
All studies show that married couples are happier than single people. Just living together without being married doesn't have the same effect.
Rank 3
FRIENDS AND FAMILY
People with large families and lots of close friends are usually happier than people who have a lot of money and live on their own.
Rank 4
NOT WANTING MORE THAN YOU'VE GOT
People who expect to have a successful career, lots of money and the perfect relationship aren't as happy as people who accept what they've got.
Rank 5
HELPING OTHERS
Studies by psychologists in different countries show that when you help other people, it's not only good for them, it also makes you feel happier.
Rank 6
RELIGION
Four out of five studies show a positive link between religion and happiness. Very religious people usually live longer too.
Rank 7
BEING ATTRACTIVE
Attractive people believe they're very happy – maybe because they also have good genes and are therefore healthier. Cosmetic surgery does not have the same effect!
Rank 8
GROWING OLD
Studies show that old people are happy as often as young people and are unhappy less often. This is probably because they spend more time doing the things they enjoy.
Rank 9
MONEY
When you're poor, money can buy you some happiness. However, when people have enough money to live comfortably, more money doesn't make them happier.
Rank 10
INTELLIGENCE
Surprisingly, this has very little effect on happiness. Being able to get on well with people is much more important than how intelligent you are.

AUXILIARIES — Trợ động từ
•
Dùng do / does cho Present Simple: Who do you work for? What does she do in her free time?
•
Dùng am, are, is cho Present Continuous: Where am I going? What are you watching? What's he doing?
•
Dùng have / has cho Present Perfect Simple: Where have you been? Has he called yet?
SUBJECT QUESTIONS — Câu hỏi về chủ ngữ
•
Hầu hết câu hỏi hỏi về tân ngữ (object): A: How many songs has Maxie written? B: He's written about 25 songs.
•
Subject questions hỏi về chủ ngữ: A: What makes her husband happy? B: Cooking makes him happy.
•
KHÔNG dùng do/does/did trong subject questions của Present/Past Simple:
✅ Who visits them most weekends?
❌ Who does visit them most weekends?
•
Subject questions có trật tự từ giống câu khẳng định. Có thể dùng: Who, What, Whose, Which.
QUESTIONS WITH PREPOSITIONS — Câu hỏi kết thúc bằng giới từ
•
Thường đặt giới từ ở cuối câu hỏi:
✅ How many countries has he been to?
✅ Who does Caroline go to art galleries with?
TIP: Thường KHÔNG đặt giới từ ở đầu: What are you talking about? (không phải: About what are you talking?)

TIPS:
• have a lie-in: when you stay in bed longer than usual in the morning. I often have a lie-in on Sundays.
• have people round for dinner: invite people to your house to have dinner. We're having Tom's parents round for dinner on Saturday.
• tidy up the house/flat: put things back in the places where you usually keep them. I usually tidy up the flat before I go to work.
• A relative is a person in your family (aunt, uncle, grandparent, cousin…). Your parents are your mother and father only.
• A gig is an informal word for a concert. I went to an amazing gig last night.
• We also use chat or have a chat to mean 'talk to someone in a friendly and informal way'.
