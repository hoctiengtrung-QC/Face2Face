Reading — Match headings to paragraphs

Read the article and match headings 1–5 to paragraphs A–E.

The world's greatest travellers
Lonely Planet
A — Nobody has changed the way we travel more than Tony and Maureen Wheeler, the founders of Lonely Planet. The company has been publishing guide books for 40 years and it sells over 5 million books a year in English. It also publishes books in seven other languages and the Lonely Planet website is one of the most popular travel sites on the internet.
B — The story began when Tony and Maureen met on a park bench in London in 1971. They got married a year later and for their honeymoon they travelled across Europe and Asia to Australia in an old van. A few months later they arrived in Sydney with only 27 cents in their pockets. Everyone they met asked them about their incredible journey, so they decided to write a book about it.
C — With the money they made from the book, Tony and Maureen spent the next 18 months travelling around southeast Asia. They wrote their second guide book, Southeast Asia on a Shoestring, in a Singapore hotel room. The book has been a best-seller since it was published in 1973 and has sold over a million copies.
D — Lonely Planet has published over 650 books since the company began and it employs around 450 people and over 200 authors. The Lonely Planet website is visited by about 5 million people every month and their internet forum, called The Thorn Tree, is one of the most popular places online for advice on travelling independently. Their television company, Lonely Planet TV, has been making programmes since 2004 and there's also a monthly Lonely Planet magazine and apps for your phone.
E — The company has certainly come a long way since Tony and Maureen's honeymoon adventure. "I think we've done a good thing," says Maureen, "and I still believe that travel is the best way for people to understand the world."

Help with Grammar
Present Perfect Continuous and Present Perfect Simple · GRAMMAR 3.2 → p134
TenseUseExample
Present Perfect ContinuousActivity that started in the past and continues in the presentThe company has been publishing guide books for 40 years.
Present Perfect SimpleState that started in the past and continues in the presentThe book has been a best-seller since 1973.
Present Perfect SimpleHow many things are finishedLonely Planet has published over 650 books.
How long ... ? and How many ... ?
• How long + Present Perfect Continuous → How long have you been waiting?
• How many + Present Perfect Simple → How many books have you read?
