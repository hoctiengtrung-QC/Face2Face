Exercise 2 — Read the article

Read about Nikki and Danny's best ever musical experiences. What was special about each one?

NIKKI WILLIAMS, USA
Five years ago I was living in New York with my cousin and I used to go to a lot of gigs. Back then I was one of Lady Gaga's biggest fans and I listened to her second album all the time. For my 21st birthday my boyfriend bought tickets to see her play live — I've never been so excited in my life! We had really good seats near the front and when Lady Gaga came onstage the whole crowd went absolutely crazy. It was a fantastic atmosphere and all the fans were singing along. That night she sang for over two hours and changed her costume about 20 times! While she was doing an encore, she threw her shoes into the crowd — and I caught one of them! My boyfriend wanted to sell it on eBay, but I wouldn't let him. Now it's my most valuable possession — and a great souvenir of a fantastic gig.
DANNY BREEN, IRELAND
When I was a teenager in Dublin I loved dance music and wanted to become a famous DJ. I practised for hours every day in my bedroom and I used to DJ at friends' parties. Then I moved to London and got a job working behind the bar at a club called Dreamworld. One Saturday night the main DJ didn't turn up and the manager asked me to play instead. Twenty minutes later I was standing in front of 1,000 people! I was incredibly nervous, but as soon as I started playing the first track I began to relax. All the clubbers were dancing and having a good time — it was the best feeling in the world. While I was playing my last track, the manager came over and congratulated me. I gave up DJing later that year because I broke my arm, but I'll never forget that amazing night!

Help with Grammar
Past Simple and Past Continuous; used to · GRAMMAR 4.1 & 4.2 → p136–137
Verb formUseExample
Past SimpleSingle completed action in the pastShe bought tickets.
Past SimpleRepeated action or habit in the pastI practised for hours every day.
Past SimpleState in the pastI was one of her biggest fans.
Past ContinuousAction in progress at a point of time in the pastI was living in New York.
Past ContinuousBackground events of a storyAll the fans were singing along.
Past ContinuousAction in progress when another action happenedWhile she was doing an encore, she threw her shoes.
used toRepeated actions, habits or states in the pastI used to go to a lot of gigs.
Used to vs Past Simple:
• used to = for repeated actions/habits/states in the past (no longer true now)
• Cannot use used to for one single action: That night she sang for over two hours. (not used to sing)
• We say: I used to go... / I didn't use to go... / Did you use to go...?

📗 GRAMMAR 4.1 — Past Simple vs Past Continuous
TenseUseExample
Past SimpleSingle completed actionShe bought tickets.
Past SimpleRepeated action/habit in the pastI practised for hours.
Past ContinuousAction in progress in the pastI was living in New York.
Past ContinuousBackground eventsAll the fans were singing.
Past ContinuousInterrupted actionWhile I was playing, the manager came.
📗 GRAMMAR 4.2 — used to
Used to:
• For repeated actions/habits/states in the past: I used to go to a lot of gigs.
• Cannot use for one single action: That night she sang. (not used to sing)
• Positive: I used to... / Negative: I didn't use to... / Question: Did you use to...?
