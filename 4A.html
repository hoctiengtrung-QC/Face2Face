📖 NIKKI WILLIAMS, USA
Five years ago...

Five years ago I was living in New York with my cousin and I used to go to a lot of gigs. Back then I was one of Lady Gaga's biggest fans and I listened to her second album all the time. For my 21st birthday my boyfriend bought tickets to see her play live – I've never been so excited in my life! We had really good seats near the front and when Lady Gaga came onstage the whole crowd went absolutely crazy.

It was a fantastic atmosphere and all the fans were singing along. That night she sang for over two hours and changed her costume about 20 times! While she was doing an encore, she threw her shoes into the crowd – and I caught one of them! My boyfriend wanted to sell it on eBay, but I wouldn't let him. Now it's my most valuable possession – and a great souvenir of a fantastic gig.

📖 DANNY BREEN, IRELAND
When I was a teenager in Dublin...

When I was a teenager in Dublin I loved dance music and wanted to become a famous DJ. I practised for hours every day in my bedroom and I used to DJ at friends' parties. Then I moved to London and got a job working behind the bar at a club called Dreamworld. One Saturday night the main DJ didn't turn up and the manager asked me to play instead.

Twenty minutes later I was standing in front of 1,000 people! I was incredibly nervous, but as soon as I started playing the first track I began to relax. All the clubbers were dancing and having a good time – it was the best feeling in the world. While I was playing my last track, the manager came over and congratulated me. I gave up DJing later that year because I broke my arm, but I'll never forget that amazing night!

📖 SOPHIE TAYLOR, UK
When I was younger...

When I was younger I was (play the violin), but I didn't use to like it very much. Then my aunt gave me a violin for my 14th birthday. I used to have violin lessons every week and I loved learning how to play a new instrument. Last year I joined the school orchestra and in March we won a music competition in our city.

The prize was the opportunity to play at the Schools Prom at the Albert Hall in London! The big night finally arrived, and while we waited to go onstage I started shaking because I was so nervous. But as soon as we got onstage I began to relax and enjoy myself. When we played the last piece of music I noticed my mum and dad in the audience. They both looked very proud!

⚙ Help with Grammar: Past Simple, Past Continuous & used to
Student Book p32 · Grammar 4.1, 4.2
⚙ Help with Grammar
Past Simple vs Past Continuous vs used to
1. Past Simple
Dùng cho: (a) hành động hoàn thành một lần trong quá khứ, (b) hành động/thói quen lặp lại trong quá khứ, (c) trạng thái trong quá khứ.
a) Single action: My boyfriend bought tickets.
b) Repeated action/habit: I practised for hours every day.
c) State: I was one of Lady Gaga's biggest fans.
2. Past Continuous
Dùng cho: (a) hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm trong quá khứ, (b) bối cảnh/nền của câu chuyện, (c) hành động đang diễn ra khi hành động khác (ngắn hơn) xảy ra.
a) Action in progress: Five years ago I was living in New York.
b) Background: All the clubbers were dancing.
c) Interrupted action: While she was doing an encore, she threw her shoes.
3. used to + base verb
Dùng để nói về hành động lặp lại hoặc trạng thái trong quá khứ mà không còn đúng ở hiện tại. Chỉ dùng cho hành động lặp lại/thói quen hoặc trạng thái — KHÔNG dùng cho một hành động đơn lẻ.
I used to go to a lot of gigs. (nhưng giờ không còn nữa)
I used to DJ at friends' parties.
❌ I used to listen to her album all the time (vẫn ổn vì là hành động lặp lại)
❌ I used to buy tickets ← SAI nếu chỉ mua một lần (hành động đơn lẻ phải dùng Past Simple)
📌 Cấu trúc used to:
Positive: subject + used to + verb → I used to go
Negative: subject + didn't use to + verb → I didn't use to like it
Question: Did + subject + use to + verb? → Did you use to go to gigs?

Trọng âm và weak form trong câu Past Continuous
she THREW her shoes into the CROWD →
While she was /wəz/ DOING an ENCORE, she THREW her shoes into the CROWD.
📌 Quy tắc:
• Trợ động từ was/were trong Past Continuous thường ở dạng weak form: was /wəz/, were /wə/ — không nhấn mạnh.
• Động từ chính (doing, throwing...) và danh từ quan trọng (encore, shoes, crowd) được nhấn mạnh (content words).
• So sánh: "Was she there?" "Yes, she WAS." — ở cuối câu, "was" chuyển sang strong form /wɒz/ khi được nhấn.
