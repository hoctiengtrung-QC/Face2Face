Help with Grammar
Present Perfect Simple: experience, unfinished past and recent events · GRAMMAR 3.1 → p133
UseExample
Experiences (time not specified)I've worked in the USA and in Europe.
Unfinished past (with for / since)I've lived here for about three years. / We've had this place since 2008.
Recent events (with just)I've just been to Banff to pick him up.
To give more info about an experienceWe use the Past Simple: I really enjoyed my time there.
been vs gone:
• been = went and came back: I've been to Banff.
• gone = went and hasn't returned: She's gone to the airport.
for / since:
• for + period of time: for three years, for a week
• since + point in time: since 2008, since last Tuesday
