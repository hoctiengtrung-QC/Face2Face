💬 Real World — Softening opinions & making generalisations
Student Book p36–37 · Real World 4.1 · Làm dịu ý kiến & đưa ra nhận định chung
🎯 Bài 4D: Người nói tiếng Anh thường làm dịu ý kiến cá nhân để tránh nghe có vẻ thô lỗ hoặc xúc phạm. Học cách dùng các từ/cụm từ giúp ý kiến trở nên nhẹ nhàng, mang tính khái quát hơn.
3aGhép câu khẳng định mạnh với câu làm dịu (SB p36, Ex 3a)
Ghép câu 1–6 (ý kiến mạnh) với câu a–f (ý kiến đã được làm dịu).
Câu gốc (mạnh)
1. Some of them can be...
2. On the whole, most...
3. Footballers tend to earn...
4. That's not very...
5. Generally speaking,...
6. Perhaps some people can...
Phần tiếp theo
a. sensible behaviour, is it?
b. rather a lot of money.
c. quite aggressive at times.
d. fans just want to see a good game.
e. take it a bit too seriously.
f. most footballers are just normal people.
📌 Đáp án: 1→c · 2→f · 3→b · 4→a · 5→d · 6→e
3bQuy tắc ngữ pháp (SB p36, Ex 3b)
📐 Quy tắc cấu trúc
After tend to we use the infinitive (to + verb)
🇻🇳 Sau "tend to" dùng động từ nguyên thể có "to"
Footballers tend to earn a lot of money.
Rather, quite, not very, a bit usually come before an adjective
🇻🇳 "rather/quite/not very/a bit" thường đứng trước tính từ
That's not very sensible. / They're quite aggressive.
We often put generally speaking and on the whole at the beginning of a sentence
🇻🇳 "generally speaking" và "on the whole" thường đứng đầu câu
On the whole, most footballers are normal people.
📌 So sánh độ lịch sự:
A. That's stupid behaviour. ← câu gốc, mạnh, có thể nghe thô lỗ
B. That's not very sensible behaviour. ← câu đã làm dịu, lịch sự hơn

• Câu B nhẹ nhàng và lịch sự hơn câu A vì dùng "not very" + tính từ tích cực (sensible) thay vì tính từ tiêu cực trực tiếp (stupid).
• not very thường đi với tính từ tích cực để tạo ý nghĩa phủ định nhẹ nhàng — KHÔNG dùng với tính từ tiêu cực.
🧰 Bảng từ/cụm từ làm dịu ý kiến
rather + adjective
🇻🇳 khá là, hơi (nhẹ)
It's rather expensive.
quite + adjective
🇻🇳 khá là
She's quite ambitious.
a bit + adjective
🇻🇳 một chút, hơi
He's a bit stubborn.
not very + (positive) adjective
🇻🇳 không hẳn là, không lắm
That's not very polite.
tend to + infinitive
🇻🇳 có xu hướng
Men tend to watch a lot of sport.
can be + adjective (at times)
🇻🇳 có thể là (đôi lúc)
Some of them can be aggressive at times.
Generally speaking, ...
🇻🇳 Nói chung là...
Generally speaking, fans just want to see a good game.
On the whole, ...
🇻🇳 Nhìn chung, về tổng thể...
On the whole, most footballers are normal people.
Perhaps + subject + can/could...
🇻🇳 Có lẽ... có thể...
Perhaps some of them can take it too seriously.

CHARLIE: Come on! Come on! Yes! What a goal!

DANIEL: Brilliant! Did you see that? Unbelievable!

REBECCA: [walking in with Lisa] Honestly, you two are obsessed. It's only a game.

CHARLIE: It's not just a game, Rebecca. This is the biggest match of the season.

LISA: I really don't understand why people get so excited about football. They're just kicking a ball around a field.

DANIEL: That's not very fair. Football's much more than that — it's about passion, tradition, community.

REBECCA: Well, I think footballers tend to earn rather a lot of money for kicking a ball around. It seems a bit ridiculous to me.

CHARLIE: On the whole, most footballers train incredibly hard. It's not as easy as it looks.

LISA: Maybe, but I remember when I was younger there was so much more violence at football matches. Fans used to get into terrible fights.

DANIEL: Generally speaking, football's a lot safer now than it used to be. There's much better security at stadiums these days.

REBECCA: Perhaps some fans can be quite aggressive at times, though.

CHARLIE: True, but on the whole most fans just want to see a good game and have a nice day out.

LISA: I just think it's strange how some people take it so seriously. It's only a game, like I said.

DANIEL: For us it's not just entertainment — it's part of who we are. Anyway, do you want a Manchester United shirt, Harry? [to baby]

REBECCA: Oh, don't give the baby a football shirt! He doesn't even support a team yet.

CHARLIE: Everyone has to support a team eventually!

✍️ Review: Apostrophes — WB p24, Ex 4
4Viết dạng đầy đủ của 's và 'd (WB p24)
Mỗi câu có 's hoặc 'd. Xác định dạng đầy đủ của nó là gì (is/has hoặc would/had).
1
It's taken three hours to get here. → has
2
I'd always thought he was sensible. →
3
He's been living in London three years. →
4
We'd recommend practical clothes for the journey. →
5
This book's published by Cambridge University Press. →
6
She'd never been skiing before. →
7
They'd get home earlier if they didn't always drive. →
8
It's the first time Simon's been to Scotland. → ,
9
I thought I'd enjoy the film because I'd loved the book. → ,
10
Paul's brother's always been ambitious. → ,
📌 Lưu ý: "'s" có thể là (1) viết tắt của "is", (2) viết tắt của "has" (trong Present Perfect), hoặc (3) sở hữu cách (possessive 's, như "Paul's brother" = "anh trai của Paul"). "'d" có thể là viết tắt của "had" (Past Perfect) hoặc "would" (Conditional).
