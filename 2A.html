■ KIM (journalist, presenter)  ·  ■ ROB (journalist, guest)
KIM:So Rob, you've read Carl Honoré's book In Praise of Slow. What's it about?
ROB:Well, it's about the Slow movement — the idea that we all work too hard and should slow down. And he's right. I must take more time off work.
KIM:Does he have any statistics about working hours?
ROB:Yes. In Germany, people now spend 15% less time at work than in 1980. And Honoré says people should only work 35 hours a week.
KIM:35 hours? That's not many.
ROB:I know! And he believes we ought to spend more time with our families.
KIM:Are there any companies that actually encourage this?
ROB:Yes! Some French employees are allowed to begin their weekend at 3pm on Thursday.
KIM:That sounds wonderful!
ROB:And people can get their best ideas when they're doing nothing. In some American companies, employees can sleep whenever they want.
KIM:Really? I'd love that!
ROB:In the UK, people are supposed to have a break every 4 hours. But Kim says that lots of people have to take work home.
KIM:That's true. I always end up working in the evenings.
ROB:And these days we're able to continue working when we're travelling. Because of smartphones and laptops.
KIM:So we never really switch off, do we?
ROB:Exactly. That's exactly what Honoré is worried about.

Exercise 4a — Grammar rules

aLook at the sentences in 3a. Complete these rules with the verb forms in bold. Use the infinitive form if necessary.

⬤⬤⬤ HELP WITH GRAMMAR — GRAMMAR 2.1
•
can and be able to → nói về khả năng hoặc khả năng xảy ra:
People can get their best ideas when they're doing nothing.
We're able to continue working when we're travelling.
•
be supposed to → nói về điều được kỳ vọng/yêu cầu:
In the UK people are supposed to have a break every four hours.
•
be allowed to and can → nói về quyền làm gì:
Some French employees are allowed to begin their weekend at 3pm on Thursday.
•
must and have to → nói điều gì là cần thiết/bắt buộc:
Rob says he must take more time off work.
Lots of people have to take work home.
•
should and ought to → đưa ra lời khuyên:
Honoré says people should only work 35 hours a week.
We ought to spend more time with our families.

Exercise 4b — Grammar questions

1. Do we use the infinitive or verb+ing after these verb forms?
✅ We use the infinitive (without to after modal verbs: must, should, can; with to after be able to, be supposed to, be allowed to, ought to, have to)

✅ be able to, be supposed to, be allowed to, ought to, have to

✅ can't / isn't/aren't able to / isn't/aren't supposed to / isn't/aren't allowed to / mustn't / don't/doesn't have to / shouldn't / ought not to

Exercise 4c — mustn't vs don't have to

cLook at these sentences. Complete the rules with mustn't or don't have to.

From the listening: conversations at the office…
❌ You mustn't send personal emails from the office. You can only send work emails.
→ mustn't = NOT allowed (prohibited)
✅ You don't have to wear a suit to work, but you can if you want to.
→ don't have to = not necessary (but you can if you want)
•
We use don't have to to say something isn't necessary.
•
We use mustn't to say something is not allowed.
Phân biệt quan trọng:
You mustn't be late. → BẮT BUỘC phải đúng giờ (vi phạm sẽ bị phạt)
You don't have to come early. → Đến sớm không cần thiết, nhưng bạn có thể nếu muốn
