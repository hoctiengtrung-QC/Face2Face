Help with Grammar
Past Perfect · GRAMMAR 4.3 → p137
TenseUseExample
Past PerfectAction that happened first in the pastCho joined the expedition after Luke had gone back to the UK.
Past PerfectTo show the order of past eventsHe read messages that people had sent him.
Past SimpleAction that happened secondCho joined the expedition.
Past Perfect vs Past Simple:
• When I turned on the TV, the programme started. (started almost immediately)
• When I turned on the TV, the programme had started. (started before I turned on)
• We often use by the time, when, as soon as, because, so with Past Perfect.
