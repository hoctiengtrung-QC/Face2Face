Student Book p20–21 · Real World 2.1
🎯 Mục tiêu bài 2D: Học cách thể hiện sự quan tâm (showing concern), đưa ra lời khuyên (giving advice) và phản hồi lời khuyên (responding to advice) trong các tình huống giao tiếp hàng ngày bằng tiếng Anh.
❤ Showing Concern — Thể hiện sự quan tâm / lo lắng
Oh, dear. What's the matter?
🇻🇳 Ồ, thế là sao. Có chuyện gì vậy?
A: I've had a terrible week. B: Oh, dear. What's the matter?
Oh, how awful!
🇻🇳 Ôi, thật khủng khiếp / tệ quá!
A: I lost my wallet. B: Oh, how awful!
Oh, what a shame.
🇻🇳 Ôi, thật tiếc / đáng tiếc quá.
A: I didn't get the job. B: Oh, what a shame.
I'm sorry to hear that.
🇻🇳 Tôi thật buồn khi nghe điều đó.
A: My cat is ill. B: I'm sorry to hear that.
I can see why you're upset.
🇻🇳 Tôi hiểu tại sao bạn buồn / khó chịu.
A: He got promoted but I didn't. B: I can see why you're upset.
Yes, I see what you mean.
🇻🇳 Vâng, tôi hiểu ý bạn / tôi thấy vấn đề.
A: The situation is really difficult. B: Yes, I see what you mean.
💡 Giving Advice — Đưa ra lời khuyên
Why don't you (+ verb)?
🇻🇳 Sao bạn không...? (gợi ý nhẹ nhàng)
Why don't you talk to him about it?
You ought to (+ verb).
🇻🇳 Bạn nên... (lời khuyên hơi nhấn mạnh)
You ought to see a doctor about that.
Have you tried (+ -ing)?
🇻🇳 Bạn đã thử... chưa?
Have you tried talking to your manager?
You should (+ verb).
🇻🇳 Bạn nên... (lời khuyên trực tiếp)
You should try to get more sleep.
If I were you, I'd (+ verb).
🇻🇳 Nếu tôi là bạn, tôi sẽ... (rất thông dụng)
If I were you, I'd take some time off.
I'd (+ verb) if I were you.
🇻🇳 Nếu là tôi, tôi sẽ... (biến thể đảo câu)
I'd write to the company if I were you.
It might be worth (+ -ing).
🇻🇳 Có thể đáng để... (gợi ý nhẹ)
It might be worth asking your boss for help.
It's (well/not) worth (+ -ing).
🇻🇳 Điều đó (rất / không) đáng để làm.
It's well worth trying. / It's not worth worrying about.
Well, it's worth a try.
🇻🇳 Thử xem sao, không mất gì cả.
A: Do you think that will work? B: Well, it's worth a try.
I'd take (+ noun/time).
🇻🇳 Tôi sẽ dành / nên dành (thời gian / cơ hội)...
I'd take some time off work and think about it.
You really must (+ verb).
🇻🇳 Bạn thực sự phải... (nhấn mạnh)
You really must talk to someone about this.
🔁 Responding to Advice — Phản hồi lời khuyên
That's a good idea.
🇻🇳 Đó là một ý hay đấy.
A: Why don't you try yoga? B: That's a good idea!
That sounds good.
🇻🇳 Nghe có vẻ hay đấy.
A: You should take the day off. B: That sounds good.
I might try that.
🇻🇳 Có thể tôi sẽ thử điều đó.
A: Have you tried drinking warm milk? B: I might try that.
Yes, I'll give it a go.
🇻🇳 Vâng, tôi sẽ thử xem. (British English)
A: Why don't you talk to her? B: Yes, I'll give it a go.
I've tried that (already).
🇻🇳 Tôi đã thử rồi.
A: Have you tried taking exercise? B: I've tried that. It doesn't help.
Yes, that might work.
🇻🇳 Vâng, cách đó có thể hiệu quả.
A: Maybe you should talk to him directly. B: Yes, that might work.
📌 Lưu ý về độ lịch sự (Politeness):
• Why don't you...? và Have you tried...? → rất lịch sự, nhẹ nhàng, phù hợp với bạn bè
• You should... và You ought to... → trực tiếp hơn, thường dùng khi ai đó hỏi ý kiến
• If I were you, I'd... → rất thông dụng, thể hiện sự đồng cảm trước khi khuyên
• Các câu phản hồi tích cực: That's a good idea / That sounds good / I might try that
• Khi từ chối lời khuyên lịch sự: I've tried that / That's a bit difficult / I'm not sure about that

Conversation 1

SARA: You look really tired, Dan. What's the matter?

DAN: I haven't been sleeping very well recently. I keep waking up in the middle of the night and I can't get back to sleep.

SARA: Oh, dear. How long has this been going on?

DAN: Yes, I've tried that. It doesn't really help.

SARA: What about some warm milk before bed?

DAN: That sounds a bit strange, but I might try that.

SARA: Well, it's worth a try. And if it doesn't work, you really ought to see a doctor.

DAN: Yes, I suppose you're right. Thanks, Sara.

Conversation 2

LUCY: Oh, Mark. What's wrong? You look terrible.

MARK: I've just had a really awful day. I got to work late because I missed my train, and then my boss was absolutely furious with me. And to make things worse, I made a stupid mistake in a report.

LUCY: Oh, how awful! I can see why you're upset. What did your boss say?

MARK: He said if it happens again, I might lose my job. I'm completely stressed out.

LUCY: Well, if I were you, I'd talk to him tomorrow and apologise properly. And maybe explain that you've been under a lot of pressure lately.

MARK: Yes, that's a good idea. I'll try that.

LUCY: And why don't you try to get to bed earlier tonight so you're not so tired tomorrow?

MARK: Yes, you're right. Thanks, Lucy.

Conversation 3

TOM: Hi, Kate. You seem a bit down today.

KATE: Yes, I'm a bit fed up, actually. I've been trying to get a promotion for ages, but my manager keeps saying it's not the right time. And I've just found out that a colleague who joined at the same time as me has just been promoted.

TOM: Oh, what a shame. I can see why you're upset. That does seem unfair.

KATE: I know. I don't know what to do.

TOM: Well, it might be worth having a proper meeting with your manager to find out what you need to do to get promoted.

KATE: Yes, I suppose that's true. I've been a bit afraid to push for it.

TOM: And if I were you, I'd also start looking at other jobs. Just to keep your options open.

KATE: That's good advice, actually. I might do that.

1aNghe và chọn đúng (SB p20)
Nghe lại và chọn True (T) hoặc False (F).
1. Dan has been having sleep problems for about three weeks.
✅ TRUE — Dan says "About three weeks now."
2. Sara suggests Dan should stop drinking tea in the evenings.
✅ FALSE — Sara suggests not drinking coffee in the evenings, not tea.
3. Mark was late to work because he missed his train.
✅ TRUE — Mark says "I got to work late because I missed my train."
4. Mark's boss told him he would lose his job immediately.
✅ FALSE — His boss said "if it happens again" he might lose his job — a warning, not immediate dismissal.
5. Kate's colleague joined the company at the same time as her.
✅ TRUE — Kate says "a colleague who joined at the same time as me."
6. Tom advises Kate not to look for other jobs.
✅ FALSE — Tom says "if I were you, I'd also start looking at other jobs."

⚙ Ngữ pháp — Modal Verbs for Advice
Student Book p21 · should / ought to / could / might
⚙ Help with Grammar
Các cách diễn đạt lời khuyên
1. should / shouldn't
Lời khuyên trực tiếp, trung tính
You should see a doctor. (Bạn nên đi khám bác sĩ.)
You shouldn't worry so much. (Bạn không nên lo lắng nhiều vậy.)
❓ Should I talk to him? (Tôi có nên nói chuyện với anh ấy không?)
2. ought to
Tương đương should, nghe trang trọng hơn một chút
You ought to apologise. (Bạn nên xin lỗi.)
She ought to get more sleep. (Cô ấy nên ngủ nhiều hơn.)
3. could
Đề xuất lịch sự, nhẹ nhàng hơn should
You could try talking to her. (Bạn có thể thử nói chuyện với cô ấy.)
You could always ask for help. (Bạn luôn có thể nhờ giúp đỡ.)
4. If I were you, I'd...
Conditional 2 — đặt mình vào hoàn cảnh người kia để khuyên; rất thông dụng trong hội thoại
If I were you, I'd talk to the manager. → rút gọn cũng nói được:
I'd talk to the manager if I were you.
5. Why don't you...?
Gợi ý lịch sự nhất — câu hỏi mang tính đề xuất
Why don't you try a different approach?
Why don't you ask your friend for advice?
6. Have you tried...? / It might be worth...
Câu hỏi/đề xuất nhẹ nhàng — sau đó dùng -ing
Have you tried meditating? (+ V-ing)
It might be worth speaking to a professional. (+ V-ing)
It's worth giving it a go. / It's not worth getting angry. (+ V-ing)
📌 Cấu trúc sau mỗi cụm từ:
• should / ought to / could + base verb (V nguyên thể không "to")
• If I were you, I'd + base verb
• Why don't you / Have you tried + verb-ing
• It might be worth / It's worth + verb-ing

SB p21 — Pronunciation practice
Nghe và lặp lại — chú ý ngữ điệu khi bày tỏ quan tâm
🎵 Ngữ điệu (Intonation) khi thể hiện quan tâm:
Khi nói Oh, dear / Oh, how awful! / I'm sorry to hear that, giọng thường xuống ở cuối — thể hiện sự đồng cảm, lo lắng thật sự.

Khi hỏi What's the matter? / What's wrong? — giọng thường lên ở cuối như câu hỏi thông thường.

Khi đưa ra lời khuyên Why don't you...? / Have you tried...? — giọng thường xuống ở cuối (không phải câu hỏi thật sự mà là đề nghị).
1Ngữ điệu lên hay xuống? (SB p21)
Chọn hướng ngữ điệu đúng cho mỗi câu.
1
"Oh, dear. What's the matter?" — rising or falling?
›
→ Oh, dear = falling ↘ (đồng cảm). What's the matter? = rising ↗ (câu hỏi thật).
2
"Oh, how awful!" — rising or falling?
›
→ Falling ↘ — thể hiện sự kinh ngạc, xót thương. Nếu giọng lên, nghe thiếu chân thành.
3
"Why don't you talk to her?" — rising or falling?
›
→ Falling ↘ — đây là đề nghị/gợi ý, không phải câu hỏi thật sự nên giọng xuống.
4
"Have you tried drinking herbal tea?" — rising or falling?
›
→ Falling ↘ — câu hỏi Yes/No thường lên ↗ nhưng ở đây là đề nghị nên giọng xuống.
5
"That's a good idea!" — rising or falling?
›
→ Falling ↘ — thể hiện sự đồng ý, tán thành. Nếu giọng lên thì nghe như đang hỏi lại.
2Trọng âm câu — Sentence stress (SB p21)
Trong mỗi câu, gạch chân từ được nhấn mạnh nhất. Sau đó xem đáp án.
1
If I WERE you, I'd TALK to him.
›
→ Nhấn: WERE và TALK. "If I were you" — "were" được nhấn vì đây là Conditional 2 (giả định). "I'd TALK" — hành động cụ thể được nhấn.
2
I can SEE why you're UPSET.
›
→ Nhấn: SEE và UPSET. Các content words (động từ chính, tính từ) được nhấn. "can" là auxiliary — weak form /kən/.
3
Have you TRIED talking to your MANAGER?
›
→ Nhấn: TRIED và MANAGER. "Have" ở đầu câu hỏi thường weak form /həv/ hoặc /əv/.

1Hội thoại mẫu (SB p21)

💬 Model Dialogue — A problem at work
ALEX:
You look a bit down today. What's the matter?
BETH:
It's my colleague, Jamie. He keeps taking credit for my ideas in meetings and I don't know what to do about it.
ALEX:
Oh, how awful! That's really unfair. I can see why you're upset.
BETH:
Yes, it's been going on for about a month now.
ALEX:
Have you tried talking to him directly about it?
BETH:
No, I haven't. I'm a bit nervous about it.
ALEX:
Well, if I were you, I'd speak to him before you go to the manager. Just explain how it makes you feel.
BETH:
That's a good idea. And what if he doesn't listen?
ALEX:
It might be worth keeping a record of your ideas — write them down with dates so you have evidence. Then you could show the manager if needed.
BETH:
Yes, that might work. Thanks, Alex. You've been really helpful.
2Thực hành roleplay (SB p21, Ex 5)
Cùng với bạn, chọn một trong các tình huống sau. Một người nói về vấn đề, người kia bày tỏ sự quan tâm và đưa ra lời khuyên. Dùng các cụm từ đã học.
🎭 Tình huống thực hành
Tình huống 1
Bạn đang có vấn đề về giấc ngủ — bạn không thể ngủ được vì lo lắng quá nhiều về công việc. Mỗi đêm bạn nằm thức đến 2-3 giờ sáng.
Gợi ý lời khuyên: try relaxation techniques / avoid screens before bed / talk to a doctor / exercise more
Tình huống 2
Bạn vừa cãi nhau với người bạn thân nhất. Cuộc tranh cãi về một điều nhỏ nhặt nhưng giờ cả hai đều chưa nói chuyện được 2 tuần.
Gợi ý lời khuyên: send a message / call them / meet face to face / write a letter / give each other some time
Tình huống 3
Bạn đang bị căng thẳng vì deadline sắp đến nhưng khối lượng công việc quá nhiều. Bạn không biết phải làm từ đâu.
Gợi ý lời khuyên: make a to-do list / ask for help / talk to your manager / work in shorter bursts / take breaks
Tình huống 4
Hàng xóm của bạn thường xuyên gây ồn ào vào ban đêm — bật nhạc to, tiệc tùng đến tận 2-3 giờ sáng.
Gợi ý lời khuyên: talk to them politely / leave a note / contact the building manager / call the police / use earplugs
💬 Cụm từ hữu ích khi roleplay
Oh, dear. What's the matter? Oh, how awful! I can see why you're upset. Have you tried...? Why don't you...? If I were you, I'd... It might be worth... That's a good idea. I might try that. I've tried that already. That sounds good. Yes, I'll give it a go.
