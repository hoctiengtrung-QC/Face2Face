📐 HELP WITH GRAMMAR
Question tags — Câu hỏi đuôi
📌 CẤU TRÚC (Structure)
•
Khẳng định + , + trợ động từ phủ định + đại từ?
You live next door to Lisa, don't you?
•
Phủ định + , + trợ động từ khẳng định + đại từ?
You haven't ordered any food yet, have you?
Main clauseQuestion tagExample
You are a studentaren't you?You're a student, aren't you?
She works heredoesn't she?She works here, doesn't she?
They have finishedhaven't they?They've finished, haven't they?
It was a good filmwasn't it?It was a good film, wasn't it?
You don't like coffeedo you?You don't like coffee, do you?
⚠️ LƯU Ý CHO NGƯỜI VIỆT (Important notes)
❌ Sai: I'm late, amn't I?
✅ Đúng: I'm late, aren't I?
•
Dùng đại từ (he, she, it, they, we, you, I) trong question tag, KHÔNG dùng tên riêng.
•
Với Let's → shall we? · Với imperative → will you?
📖 Barbara went to Liverpool University, didn't she? (KHÔNG: didn't Barbara?)

Speaking Practice
Meeting new people · Asking and answering with question tags
💬 Model conversation
You live next door to Lisa, don't you? → Yes, I do.
You're from London originally, aren't you? → Yes, that's right.
You've been diving, haven't you? → No, I haven't, actually. It sounds a bit too dangerous.
💡 TIP: When the information isn't correct, use "actually" to sound more polite: No, I haven't, actually.
🗣️ Work in pairs. Ask and answer:

Help with Pronunciation
-ed adjectives: số âm tiết và trọng âm
🔹 1 âm tiết
🔹 2 âm tiết
🔹 3 âm tiết
📌 Mẹo phát âm -ed: Nếu từ kết thúc bằng /t/ hoặc /d/ → thêm 1 âm tiết (excited /ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/). Nếu không → -ed không thêm âm tiết (stressed /strest/).

Question tags to check information
FunctionExample
Checking information (positive statement)You live next door to Lisa, don't you?
Checking information (negative statement)You haven't ordered any food yet, have you?
Short answer (correct)Yes, I do. / Yes, she did. / Yes, that's right.
Short answer (incorrect, polite)No, I haven't, actually.
📝 Lưu ý: Dùng Yes, that's right thay cho Yes, I am / Yes, I do khi muốn xác nhận thông tin một cách tự nhiên.
