DANIEL:I'm an architect. What about you?
CHARLIE:I teach people how to sail. And I also work for a package tour company.
DANIEL:Oh, that sounds great. You must travel a lot, don't you?
CHARLIE:Yes, I do. All over the world, actually.
LISA:You both support Manchester United, don't you?
REBECCA:Yes, we do! Are you a fan, Daniel?
DANIEL:I'm afraid not. I'm an Arsenal supporter!
CHARLIE:Oh dear! Never mind. You've got a son called Harry, haven't you, Rebecca?
REBECCA:That's right. He's just started school.
DANIEL:You haven't ordered any food yet, have you? I'm starving!
LISA:No, we haven't. Let's get some menus!

⬤⬤⬤ REAL WORLD — Question tags (REAL WORLD 1.1)
MỤC ĐÍCH
•
Dùng question tags (don't you?, wasn't it?…) để kiểm tra thông tin mà ta cho là đúng / tìm thông tin mới.
CẤU TRÚC
•
Dùng auxiliary/modal verb chính trong question tag:
You live next door to Lisa, don't you?
•
Chỉ dùng đại từ (pronouns) trong question tags — KHÔNG dùng tên người:
Barbara went to Liverpool University, didn't she?
•
Mệnh đề chính khẳng định → question tag phủ định:
✅ It was a great match yesterday, wasn't it?
•
Mệnh đề chính phủ định → question tag khẳng định:
✅ You haven't ordered any food yet, have you?
SHORT ANSWERS
•
Khi thông tin ĐÚNG → dùng câu trả lời ngắn khẳng định:
A: You live here, don't you?   B: Yes, I do.
•
Khi thông tin SAI → dùng actually để lịch sự hơn:
A: You've been diving, haven't you?   B: No, I haven't, actually. It sounds a bit too dangerous to me.
TIP: Cũng có thể dùng Yes, that's right.:
A: You're from London originally, aren't you?   B: Yes, that's right. / Yes, I am.
TIP đặc biệt — Present Simple & Past Simple:
Nếu main verb là Present Simple hoặc Past Simple → dùng don't, doesn't hoặc didn't:
Jim lives in the USA, doesn't he?  |  She left early, didn't she?
Dùng aren't I? (KHÔNG dùng amn't I?): I'm late, aren't I?

📌 Tóm tắt quy tắc
•
Question tags dùng để kiểm tra thông tin ta cho là đúng.
•
Dùng auxiliary trong tag: You live here, don't you?
•
Chỉ dùng pronoun: Barbara left, didn't she?
•
Khẳng định → tag phủ định: It's nice, isn't it?
•
Phủ định → tag khẳng định: It isn't nice, is it?
•
Dùng actually khi thông tin sai: No, I haven't, actually.
•
Dùng aren't I? KHÔNG dùng amn't I?: I'm late, aren't I?
Cũng dùng được: Yes, that's right.
A: You're from London originally, aren't you?
B: Yes, that's right. / Yes, I am.
