💬 Real World — Asking for & Making Recommendations
Student Book p28–29 · Real World 3.1 · Gợi ý và đề xuất địa điểm
🎯 Bài 3D: Học cách hỏi xin gợi ý (asking for recommendations), gợi ý (recommending), không gợi ý (not recommending), và phản hồi gợi ý trong ngữ cảnh du lịch.
❓ Asking for recommendations — Hỏi xin gợi ý
Do you know any good places to stay/eat?
🇻🇳 Bạn có biết chỗ nào tốt để ở/ăn không?
Ella: Do you know any good places to stay in Delhi?
What's the best way to (get around)?
🇻🇳 Cách tốt nhất để (di chuyển) là gì?
Mike: What's the best way to get around the city?
What else is worth visiting/seeing?
🇻🇳 Còn điều gì đáng ghé thăm/xem không?
Ella: What else is worth visiting near Delhi?
What about (places outside Delhi)?
🇻🇳 Còn (những địa điểm ngoài Delhi) thì sao?
Mike: What about places to see outside the city?
Have you got any other tips?
🇻🇳 Bạn có thêm mẹo/lời khuyên nào không?
Ella: Have you got any other tips for our trip?
✅ Recommending things — Gợi ý
It's probably best to (use rickshaws).
🇻🇳 Có lẽ tốt nhất là (dùng xe kéo).
Charlie: It's probably best to use rickshaws to get around.
I'd recommend (the trains).
🇻🇳 Tôi sẽ gợi ý (tàu hỏa).
Rebecca: I'd recommend the trains — they're cheap and reliable.
You should definitely see (the Red Fort).
🇻🇳 Bạn nhất định phải xem (Pháo đài Đỏ).
Charlie: You should definitely see the Red Fort in Old Delhi.
That's well worth seeing/visiting.
🇻🇳 Điều đó rất đáng xem/ghé thăm.
Rebecca: The Taj Mahal? That's well worth seeing — go in the early morning.
You really must go to (Agra) to see (the Taj Mahal).
🇻🇳 Bạn nhất định phải đến (Agra) để xem (Taj Mahal).
Charlie: You really must go to Agra to see the Taj Mahal.
❌ Not recommending things — Không gợi ý
Don't bother going to (the museums).
🇻🇳 Đừng mất công đến (các bảo tàng).
Rebecca: Don't bother going to the museums — they're quite boring.
It isn't really worth visiting (I don't think).
🇻🇳 Không đáng ghé thăm đâu (tôi nghĩ vậy).
Charlie: That area? It isn't really worth visiting, I don't think.
Don't drink anything with ice in it.
🇻🇳 Đừng uống bất cứ thứ gì có đá.
Rebecca: Don't drink anything with ice in it — you might get ill.
I wouldn't eat any salads.
🇻🇳 Tôi sẽ không ăn các món salad.
Charlie: I wouldn't eat any salads — food hygiene can be a problem.
🔁 Responding to recommendations — Phản hồi gợi ý
That's good to know.
🇻🇳 Tốt khi biết điều đó.
Ella: That's good to know — we'll take a guide with us.
That sounds good.
🇻🇳 Nghe có vẻ hay đấy.
Mike: The Taj Mahal? That sounds wonderful!
Thanks, that's really useful.
🇻🇳 Cảm ơn, điều đó thực sự hữu ích.
Ella: Thanks, that's really useful. We'll definitely try the rickshaws.
Yes, I've heard that before.
🇻🇳 Vâng, tôi đã nghe điều đó rồi.
Mike: The ice? Yes, I've heard that before.
📌 Lưu ý cấu trúc (SB p29, Ex 3b):
• It's (well/not) worth + V-ing: It's worth visiting. / It's not worth going there.
• Don't bother + V-ing: Don't bother going to the museums.
• I'd/I wouldn't + base verb: I'd recommend the trains. / I wouldn't eat salads.

⚙ Ngữ pháp — Verb forms after Real World phrases
Student Book p29 · Real World 3.1 · Cấu trúc động từ theo sau cụm từ
⚙ Real World 3.1 — Cấu trúc động từ
What verb form follows each phrase?
1. Phrases followed by INFINITIVE (base verb):
It's probably best to use rickshaws.
I'd recommend the trains.
You should definitely see the Red Fort.
You really must go to Agra.
I wouldn't eat any salads.
2. Phrases followed by VERB-ING:
It's (well/not) worth visiting.
Don't bother going to the museums.
That's well worth seeing.
📌 Tóm tắt:
• It's probably best to / I'd recommend / You should / You really must / I wouldn't + base verb
• It's worth / It's not worth / Don't bother / That's well worth + verb-ing
1Điền dạng động từ đúng (SB p29, Ex 3a)
1
It's probably best rickshaws. (use)
2

3

4

5

6
Don't bother to the museums. (go)
7
